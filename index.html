Engineering Approach

Define trust boundaries

Derive identity from verified credentials and resolve permissions from server-controlled data.

Plan for failure

Specify what happens when a dependency is unavailable. For an authorization service, make denial and audit behavior explicit.

Make decisions traceable

Record why an operation was accepted, blocked, or sent back for approval so it can be investigated later.
